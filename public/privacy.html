Your information

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Short version: we only ask for what we need to book and complete your detail. We do not sell your information, and we use it only to take care of your vehicle and your booking.

Who we are

VVM Details is a mobile car wash and detailing business based in Chesapeake Beach, MD, serving DC, MD and VA. You can reach us at vvm.detailing@gmail.com or (240) 601-9451.

What we collect

When you book, we ask for:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your vehicle (year, make and model)
  • The address where we should arrive, and any concerns or questions you share
  • The service, date and time you choose
  • Your ZIP code, to work out any travel fee and the family area discount, and whether you chose drop-off
  • Any discount or referral code you use
  • Whether you said yes to emails about offers and news (only if you tick the box)

If you have a Garage account, we also keep your own referral code and how many friends have used it.

We do not collect payment details on this website. We take payment after we complete your service.

How we use it

  • To schedule your appointment and confirm the details with you
  • To travel to you and complete your service
  • To call, text or email you about your booking
  • To work out travel fees and discounts
  • To email you a confirmation, and a reminder the day before your booking
  • To email you offers and news, only if you said yes (you can stop at any time)
  • To keep records of our work and handle payment

Who handles it for us

We use a few trusted services to run the business. They only receive what they need to do their job.

  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • Supabase briefly holds a booked time so no one else can pick it (up to 3 days).
  • Resend delivers your booking request email to us.
  • Brevo sends you booking confirmations and reminders, and our offers and news if you said yes.
  • Square keeps our appointments, customer records and takes payment after your service.
  • Gmail receives our booking emails.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on this site, so your browser contacts Google when the page loads.

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this page.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as we need them to serve you, keep our business records and meet any legal or tax requirements. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, and we will do so unless we are required to keep it.

Your choices

You can contact us any time to see, correct or delete the information we have about you, or to stop receiving texts or emails from us. Every offers email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also just tell us.

Children

This website is meant for adults booking a service. It is not directed to children under 13.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website or service is perfectly secure, so please share only what is needed.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Email vvm.detailing@gmail.com or call or text (240) 601-9451.
