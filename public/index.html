The full guide

Paint Enhancement + 5 Year Ceramic Coating

What is Paint Enhancement?

Paint enhancement, also called paint correction, is the step that makes your paint look new again. Everyday washing, dust and sun leave fine swirl marks, light scratches and haze on the surface. These tiny defects scatter light, so the paint looks dull instead of deep and glossy.

We use a machine polisher and professional polishing compounds to carefully level a microscopic layer of clear coat. The surface becomes smooth and flat, so light reflects evenly. That is the depth, clarity and mirror-like shine you see.

  • Removes swirl marks and fine scratches
  • Restores gloss, depth and clarity
  • Prepares the paint to bond with the coating
  • Clears haze and light oxidation

What is a Ceramic Coating?

A ceramic coating is a liquid nano-ceramic layer, made from silica, that we apply by hand to your paint after it has been corrected. As it cures, it forms a hard, transparent, super glossy layer that chemically bonds to the clear coat.

Wax and sealants sit on top of the paint and wear away. A ceramic coating bonds with the surface, so it does not wash off. Ours is a 5 year coating.

What it does

Chemical Bond Layer

Bonds to the clear coat instead of sitting on it, so it does not wash away.

Hydrophobic

Water beads up and rolls off, pulling dirt with it.

Stays Cleaner Longer

A slick surface makes it harder for dirt, dust and road film to stick, so washes are faster and easier.

Repels Contaminants

Bird droppings, bug splatter and tree sap sit on the coating, not directly on your paint, so they are easier to remove.

Protects Your Paint

Adds a hard layer that helps defend against UV rays, oxidation and light chemical staining.

Super Glossy Reflection

A deep, mirror-like shine that lasts for years, not weeks.

A ceramic coating makes your vehicle easier to keep clean and helps protect the paint. It is not scratch-proof, and it still needs regular washing. We will show you exactly how to care for it.

The coating locks in whatever is under it.

A ceramic coating bonds to the paint so strongly that it stays for years. That is great for protection, but it also means any swirls, scratches or haze left on the paint get sealed in with it. Fixing them later would mean removing the coating. That is why we always correct the paint first. It is what makes the finish flawless for the next 5 years.

Our process

  1. 1
    Wash & decontaminate

    Pre-wash, PH-balanced foam and a careful hand wash, then iron remover and a clay treatment to lift embedded contamination.

  2. 2
    Paint correction

    A one-step machine polish removes the majority of swirls and defects. Two-step available for older vehicles or more severe condition.

  3. 3
    Panel prep

    Each panel is cleaned and prepped so the coating bonds directly to the paint.

  4. 4
    Ceramic coating

    The 5 year ceramic coating is applied by hand, panel by panel, and cures into a hard, glossy layer.

  5. 5
    Final walkthrough

    We review every step with you and quality-check the finish. Ready to correct any request.
