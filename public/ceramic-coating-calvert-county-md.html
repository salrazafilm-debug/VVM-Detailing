Questions, answered

What is paint enhancement?

Paint enhancement, also called paint correction, is the step that makes your paint look new again. We use a machine polisher and professional compounds to carefully level a microscopic layer of clear coat, which removes swirl marks, fine scratches and haze.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

Ours is a 5 year coating. Wax lasts weeks to a few months, and our free sealant lasts about 3 months.

Is a ceramic coating scratch-proof?

No. It makes your vehicle easier to keep clean and helps protect the paint, but it is not scratch-proof and still needs regular washing. A scratch that goes through the clear coat cannot be polished out, and we will tell you what is realistic for your paint.
