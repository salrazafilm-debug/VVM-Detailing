Questions, answered

Do you service all of Calvert County?

Yes. We are based in Chesapeake Beach and come to homes, workplaces, docks and marinas across Calvert County. Our family area covers Chesapeake Beach, North Beach, Huntingtown, Owings, Dunkirk and Prince Frederick.

How much is travel?

Travel is free up to 30 minutes from us. Farther trips have a small travel fee that you see before you book. Customers in our family area never pay a travel fee.

What is the family area discount?

Customers in the Calvert County family area get FREE travel plus $25 off their total price. It applies automatically when you enter your ZIP code at checkout.

Do you also serve DC, Maryland and Virginia?

Yes. Calvert County is our home, and we also travel to the DC, MD and VA area. A travel fee applies for longer trips, and you see it before you book.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

No. There is no deposit. We take payment only after we complete your service.

What should I do before you arrive?

Please remove all personal belongings and any items from your vehicle so we can complete the detail. Park where we have room to open the doors and work around the vehicle, and let us know if you have water and power access.

Can I drop my vehicle off instead?

Yes. You can drop off at our home base in Chesapeake Beach, MD. The address is shown when you choose drop-off while booking, and there is no travel fee for drop-off.
