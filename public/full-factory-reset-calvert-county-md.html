Questions, answered

Is the ceramic sealant really free?

Yes. The Full Factory Reset includes a free ceramic sealant that lasts about 3 months. It is not the same as our 5 year ceramic coating, which is a separate service.

Can I add things to the Full Factory Reset?

Yes. Pet hair removal, seat shampoo, stain removal and odor removal can be added, and so can Engine Bay Detail or Headlight Restoration.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size and condition of the vehicle. The booking page shows the estimated time for your size before you pick a day.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

No. There is no deposit. We take payment only after we complete your service.

Where do you work?

We come to you anywhere in Calvert County, including Chesapeake Beach, North Beach, Prince Frederick, Huntingtown, Dunkirk and Owings, and we also travel around the DC, MD and VA area.
