# Upload your photos and videos here

Put files in this folder (`assets/uploads/`) and they show up on the website automatically. No code changes needed.
Use the exact file names below. The extension can be `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png` or `.webp` for photos, and `.mp4`, `.webm` or `.mov` for video.

## How to upload from your phone or computer

1. Open the repo on GitHub: `salrazafilm-debug/VVM-Details`
2. Go into the `assets` folder, then the `uploads` folder
3. Tap **Add file > Upload files**, choose your photos or videos, then **Commit changes**
4. The website updates after the next deploy (about a minute after the site updates)

You can also just send the files to Claude in chat and ask for them to be added.

## Tips

- Photos: JPG or PNG, ideally under 500 KB each. Phone photos can be big, so shrink them first (any "resize image" app works).
- iPhone: HEIC photos do not display in browsers. Set Camera > Formats > **Most Compatible**, or export as JPG.
- Video: MP4 (H.264), 10 to 30 seconds, under 25 MB. GitHub's website upload limit is 25 MB per file.
  For longer videos, upload to YouTube and ask Claude to embed it.
- Keep names lowercase with dashes, no spaces.
